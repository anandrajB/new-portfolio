### Krediq – Digital Invoice Financing & Lending Platform

**Krediq** is a fintech platform focused on collateral-free invoice financing and vendor financing for MSMEs. The platform enables businesses to unlock working capital through invoice discounting, reverse factoring, digital onboarding, credit assessment, loan management, and payment tracking. It leverages GST invoice data and trade relationships to provide faster access to credit.
